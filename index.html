VETERAN LEAD SCRIPT Hey (name), this is just (agent name). I had a second to get back to you. I’m the one responsible for handling your Veteran Life Insurance form. Verify DOB, email, branch of service, and thank them for their service. Ask: What was the main reason you inquired — to leave money behind for your family or simply take care of final expenses? Are we taking care of just you today, or you and your spouse? How many life insurance policies do you currently have? If yes: Are you looking to increase coverage or see if you can lower your current cost? If none: No worries — we’ll see what you may qualify for today. Complete approved health questions and needs analysis. Present three suitable options clearly and review coverage amount, premium and fit with the client's goals and budget. Once selected: complete the eApp, confirm beneficiaries, draft date, coverage and premium. Ascend compliance note: use accurate carrier/rating/pricing statements only. Do not use unsupported statistics or discourage a client from seeking another review.
AGED VETERAN LEAD SCRIPT Hey (name), this is just (agent name). I had a second to get back to you. I’m the one responsible for helping with the Veteran Life Insurance form. I’m sure you’ve gotten a lot of calls — did you find something, or what ended up happening? If they already got coverage: Ask which carrier they chose so the file can be updated. If they want a comparison, verify current coverage, premium, health and goals before presenting alternatives. If they still need coverage: Verify DOB, email and branch of service. Ask the main reason for coverage, whether spouse coverage is needed, and what current coverage exists. Proceed to approved carrier/application workflow and needs analysis. Ascend compliance note: never disparage another carrier or promise savings before verifying the facts.
